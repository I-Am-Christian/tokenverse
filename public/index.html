FLIGHT MANUAL / 002

How to survive the Outer Rim.

01 / FLIGHT

WASD or arrows move your ship. Click asteroids to autopilot. On phones, use the direction pad.

02 / MINE & TRADE

Approach rocks to mine automatically. Return to the central port to sell raw ore, or refine it into simulated material tokens. Daily token rewards are capped.

03 / PvP COMBAT

Outside the central protected ring, press SPACE or FIRE to shoot. Click a rival to aim at them. Defeat drops 25% of raw cargo, never wallet tokens.

04 / STATIONS

Craft an outpost using ore, $MOON, and $JUNK. Stations persist on the server, provide storage/refining, and may be attacked by rivals when their owner is online. Stored Neonite feeds defensive turrets.

This is a multiplayer tech demo, not a crypto launch. Market prices and rewards are simulated and cannot be withdrawn. Guest pilot identities are not secure enough to store real assets.
