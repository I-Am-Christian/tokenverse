# Tokenverse — Brand direction

- **Name:** Tokenverse
- **Website proposal:** tokenverse.online (not registered or availability-verified)
- **GitHub repository target:** I-Am-Christian/Tokenverse (not yet created)
- **Tagline:** Mine. Trade. Conquer.
- **Genre:** persistent multiplayer space mining, PvP, station building, simulated multi-token economy
- **Current version:** multiplayer alpha 0.2
- **Theme:** near-black space, neon cyan and violet, orbiting planet icon
- **Economy:** $MOON, $NEON, $DUST and $VOID are simulated demo balances, not real cryptocurrency.

Do not present the demo as a live crypto exchange or a production-ready money game.
